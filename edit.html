    Go-live time
    Choose a time between 1 hour and 90 days from now. Your posting goes live within 15 minutes after that time, and its closing date is counted from the moment it actually goes live, not from now. Until then it is a scheduled draft that candidates cannot see; you can change the time, remove it or publish it now from My postings.
