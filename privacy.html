Privacy

FightGhostJobs is a registry of job postings disclosed by employers. This page says what we keep about you and why.

If you look up postings

To search, you verify an email address by clicking a link we send you. We keep that address and the date you verified it, so you do not have to verify again for 90 days. We do not show your address to employers or to anyone else, and we do not send you anything but the links you ask for.

We record which postings were shown to you in each search, which ones you opened, and each time you ask for an apply link, together with the verified identity that asked. We keep these records so the registry's numbers are honest and so an abused apply link can be traced back. They are not sold or shared.

If you comment on a posting, we keep the comment, the time and your verified identity. Other candidates and the employer see the comment and the time, never who wrote it. You cannot edit or delete a comment once it is posted; we hide a comment when a report about it is upheld, and the hidden text stays in our records. A report you file (about a comment, or about a link that led somewhere wrong) is kept with your identity and read only by us.

If you register postings

We keep your name, your work email address, the organization you belong to, each sign-in, each change to your organization's roster, and every change you make to a posting, with the note you wrote. Candidates see the posting's disclosed facts, never your name or address.

We email you about the postings and rosters you are responsible for, and about nothing else: when an admin adds you to a roster, when a posting of yours is five days and one day from closing, when it expires, when a scheduled posting goes live or could not be published, and at most once every six hours when candidates leave new comments on it. These emails carry the posting's title and postID, never its description, a comment's text or an apply link. They come from noreply@fightghostjobs.com; replying reaches sales@fightghostjobs.com. There is no unsubscribe for these operational messages yet; every sent email is logged and the log is pruned after 90 days.

Cookies

We set no cookies of our own. Your sign-in is kept in your browser's local storage and ends when you sign out. The provider that fronts our API (Cloudflare) sets one short-lived bot-protection cookie, __cf_bm, on the API's own domain for about 30 minutes; it does not identify you to us and we do not read it.

Who processes the data

The registry runs on Supabase (database, sign-in and functions), with Cloudflare in front of it; the site is served by GitHub Pages; sign-in links are sent through Resend. Place names come from GeoNames and the U.S. Census Bureau.

How long

We keep these records for as long as the registry exists. Rate-limiting records are deleted within days. To ask about your data, write to privacy@fightghostjobs.com.
